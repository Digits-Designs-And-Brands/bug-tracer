# Privacy Policy for Bug Tracer Chrome Extension

**Last Updated: [Date]**

## Overview

Bug Tracer is a Chrome extension designed to help developers and teams create comprehensive bug reports through screen recording and data capture. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data and what information is collected, stored, and shared.

## Data Collection

### What We Collect

**During Recording Sessions Only:**
- Screen recordings (video and audio)
- Console logs from web pages
- Network requests and responses
- Page URLs and metadata
- Recording timestamps and duration

**Configuration Data:**
- Upload provider settings (stored locally in Chrome sync)
- Extension preferences and settings

### What We DON'T Collect

- Personal information or user accounts
- Browsing history outside of recording sessions
- Data from non-recording sessions
- Analytics or usage tracking
- Any data when the extension is not actively recording

## Data Storage

### Local Storage (Default)
- All recordings are stored locally on your device using IndexedDB
- Console logs and network data are stored locally
- Configuration settings are stored in Chrome's sync storage
- No data is transmitted to external servers without your explicit action

### Cloud Storage (Optional)
- Upload to cloud providers only occurs when you explicitly choose to upload
- You control which provider to use (Cloudinary, AWS S3, or custom endpoints)
- Upload is completely optional and user-initiated

## Data Sharing

### No Automatic Sharing
- We do not automatically share any data with third parties
- No data is sent to our servers or external services without your permission
- All data remains on your device unless you choose to upload

### Optional Cloud Upload
When you choose to upload recordings:
- **Cloudinary**: Data is sent to Cloudinary's servers according to their privacy policy
- **AWS S3**: Data is sent to your configured AWS S3 bucket
- **Custom Endpoints**: Data is sent to your specified endpoint

### Third-Party Services
- We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services
- We do not share data with marketing or advertising companies
- The only third-party interactions are with upload providers you configure

## User Control

### Data Collection Control
- Recording only occurs when you explicitly start a recording session
- You can stop recording at any time
- No background data collection or monitoring

### Data Storage Control
- All recordings are stored locally by default
- You can delete recordings at any time through the extension interface
- You can clear all local data by uninstalling the extension

### Upload Control
- Upload is completely optional
- You choose which recordings to upload
- You choose which provider to use
- You can delete uploaded recordings through the provider's interface

## Security

### Local Data Security
- Data is stored using Chrome's secure storage mechanisms
- No unencrypted data transmission
- Provider credentials are stored securely in Chrome sync

### Upload Security
- Uploads use HTTPS encryption
- Provider credentials are handled securely
- No credentials are stored in plain text

## Data Retention

### Local Data
- Recordings are stored locally until you delete them
- No automatic deletion or expiration
- You have full control over data retention

### Cloud Data
- Cloud data retention depends on your chosen provider's policies
- You can delete cloud data through the provider's interface
- We do not control cloud provider data retention

## Children's Privacy

Bug Tracer is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by:
- Updating the "Last Updated" date
- Posting the updated policy in the extension
- Notifying users through the extension interface
