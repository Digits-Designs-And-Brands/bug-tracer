## Detailed Description

### 🐛 Bug Tracer: Professional Bug Reporting Made Simple

Transform your bug reporting workflow with Bug Tracer - the ultimate Chrome extension for developers, QA teams, and product managers. Capture comprehensive bug reports with screen recordings, console logs, and network requests in one seamless workflow.

### ✨ Key Features

**🎥 Advanced Screen Recording**
- High-quality screen and audio capture
- Automatic tab/window selection
- Real-time recording with duration tracking

**📊 Comprehensive Data Capture**
- **Console Logs**: Automatically capture all console output (log, error, warn, info, debug)
- **Network Requests**: Monitor fetch and XHR requests with full request/response details
- **Page Context**: Capture URL, timestamp, and page metadata

**☁️ Multi-Provider Upload Support**
- **Cloudinary**: Free tier with 25GB storage, automatic video optimization
- **AWS S3**: Enterprise-grade storage with flexible pricing
- **Generic HTTP**: Use your own upload endpoints and APIs

**💾 Local-First Storage**
- All recordings stored locally using IndexedDB
- No data sent externally without your permission
- Privacy-focused design

### 🚀 Perfect For

- **Developers**: Document bugs with complete context
- **QA Teams**: Create detailed test reports
- **Product Managers**: Share user experience issues
- **Support Teams**: Capture user-reported problems

### 🔒 Privacy & Security

- **Local Storage**: All data stays on your device by default
- **Optional Upload**: Choose when and where to share recordings
- **No Tracking**: No analytics or user tracking
- **Secure**: Provider credentials stored securely in Chrome sync

### 🛠️ Easy Setup

1. Install the extension
2. Click the extension icon
3. Start recording and reproduce the bug
4. Stop recording and optionally upload to your preferred provider
5. Share the link with your team

- **Flexible**: Multiple upload providers to fit your workflow
- **Privacy-First**: Your data, your control
- **Open Source**: Transparent, community-driven development
